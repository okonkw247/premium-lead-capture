Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Adams X Project ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit adamsxproject.com.ng or interact with our services, including our waitlist, free resources, and paid programs sold through Whop.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you join our waitlist, download a free resource, or purchase a program, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Payment information (processed securely by Whop — we do not store your card details)
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information via cookies and tracking pixels (including the Meta Pixel and Whop Pixel)

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

  • Send you emails about our waitlist, programs, and updates
  • Deliver purchased products and program access
  • Improve our website and marketing based on aggregated, anonymized data
  • Run advertising campaigns and measure their performance through tracking pixels

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

COOKIES AND TRACKING

We use tracking technologies, including the Meta Pixel and Whop Pixel, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools may collect data about your browsing behavior on our site.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party services to operate our business:

  • Whop (payment processing and community hosting)
  • Email automation tools (for sending waitlist and program emails)
  • Notion (for delivering tracking templates)
  • Meta/Facebook (for advertising and analytics)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. You can request that we delete your personal information by contacting us directly.

DATA RETENTION

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
