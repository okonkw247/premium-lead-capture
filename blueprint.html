Why I built this

The problem with most productivity advice is that it assumes you're already consistent.

Most systems are designed for people who are already showing up. What they give you is optimization — a better morning routine, a tighter schedule, a new app to track your habits. None of that is useful if you keep starting over every three weeks.

The Comeback framework was built for the person who keeps stopping. Who has the goals, the ideas, the drive on a good day — but can't make the execution stick past the initial wave of motivation.

The Blueprint is where I start explaining this. It's free because the first step is understanding the problem clearly. Once you see it, the rest makes sense.
